# Learning design

The course is designed around understanding a situation and communicating something useful.
The twelve stages are preparation units and CEFR-labelled target slices, not evidence that a
short course covers or certifies an entire CEFR level. There is no A3. Pre-B1 connects A2
everyday transactions to explaining causes, correcting misunderstandings and negotiating plans.

## Learning loop

1. Preview relevant words and grammar in meaningful sentences.
2. Recall a small group of words before revealing them; use each in a different sentence.
3. Read a story and listen to a distinct message. Answer questions about meaning and action.
4. Plan a spoken response with key words, record it, listen back and improve one thing.
5. Write for a recipient and purpose. Compare with a complete example or a clearly labelled excerpt.
6. Apply the skills to separate final tasks. Each skill must meet its own course criteria.

Five-word recall rounds deliberately ask for learner self-assessment, accept that synonyms can
be valid, and offer another round of the items the learner found difficult. They do not award
mastery, alter test results, or pretend to provide a validated spaced-repetition schedule.
The short-round format is a product choice. Retrieval-practice research motivates recall before
restudy, but this application's effects on learning have not yet been measured.

The communication coach adds clarification, slower-speech requests, checking understanding,
polite disagreement and summarising an agreement. Its prompts progress from short familiar
phrases to qualification and negotiation. Existing appointment, shopping, lunch and course-message
missions remain available as additional interactive role-play, with their own difficulty.
They do not bypass the staged assessment gates.

## Support and independent evidence

Dutch remains the practice language. English and/or Persian are selectable support languages.
Changing support mode preserves the current work. Persian text has its own language and direction.
Final reading/listening answers, source translations, listening transcripts and model answers
are withheld by the server; task instructions remain understandable. Submitted final work is
immutable during a retry so a failed provider response cannot turn into a changed examination.

Recognition transcripts help assess communicated content. They do not establish accent,
pronunciation, prosody or fluency quality. The interface tells learners this and supports listening
back. Language-model feedback remains provisional and source content awaits qualified review.

## Design choices

- One visible next step, separate four-skill progress, and a clear prerequisite explanation.
- A restrained navy/blue palette; high-contrast text; generous reading width; fewer technical labels.
- Support tools live inside the relevant practice section rather than adding more main navigation.
- A single native audio player prevents overlapping playback and exposes pause/replay controls.
- Recording and submission protect navigation; drafts and submitted payloads support recovery.
- A self-report never unlocks a stage. Admin previews never become student passes.
- Fixed teaching language, course policies and scenario facts remain versioned application data.

## Sources and validation boundaries

- [Council of Europe: key concepts](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/key-concepts)
  and [mediation](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/mediation):
  action, interaction, learner agency and making meaning are reference points for task design.
- [Karpicke and Roediger, 2008, The Critical Importance of Retrieval for Learning](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1152408):
  primary research supporting retrieval as a learning activity; it does not validate this software.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): accessibility target. Automated sampled checks do not
  replace a complete manual accessibility evaluation.

Before making effectiveness claims, evaluate independent transfer to new scenarios, delayed recall,
help used, correction accuracy and actual learner experience. Completion and app usage alone do not
demonstrate learning. See CURRICULUM.md for content coverage and VALIDATION_REPORT.md for software evidence.

Generated video remains a future reviewed asset type, with captions and transcripts. No video
provider, empty video navigation or extra cloud bill is introduced by this release.
