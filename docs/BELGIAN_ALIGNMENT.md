# Belgian Dutch learning and exam alignment

Source check: **12 October 2026**. Data release: **2026.10.12.1**.

The platform keeps twelve open practice stages. Six use the CEFR levels A1–C2;
Pre-A1 uses the descriptor band added in the CEFR Companion volume. The five
other `pre-` stages are our learning bridges. They are not additional official
levels, exam certificates or Flemish course modules. There is no A3 stage.

The mapping below guides task design. It does **not** establish that every
existing exercise has the stated difficulty. The current vocabulary, story and
topic banks remain unreviewed. A bank containing 500 words or 100 topics does not
become a calibrated CEFR course by meeting those counts.

## Reference map

| Practice stage | Reference status | Flemish NT2 reference | Design focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-A1 | CEFR descriptor band | No equivalent module asserted | Recognise familiar words; give basic personal information with support. |
| A1 | CEFR level | Breakthrough, commonly 1.1 | Short explicit messages and simple exchanges. |
| pre-A2 | Internal A1 → A2 bridge | No equivalent module asserted | Link a request to a reason and compare simple options. |
| A2 | CEFR level | Waystage, commonly 1.2 | Handle familiar everyday requests and problems. |
| pre-B1 | Internal A2 → B1 bridge | No equivalent module asserted | Connected accounts, clarification and one unexpected complication. |
| B1 | CEFR level | Richtgraad 2 | Independent familiar communication with reasons and connected text. |
| pre-B2 | Internal B1 → B2 bridge | No equivalent module asserted | Compare viewpoints, support a choice and answer an objection. |
| B2 | CEFR level | Richtgraad 3 | Analyse arguments and communicate clearly in work or study. |
| pre-C1 | Internal B2 → C1 bridge | No equivalent module asserted | Implicit meaning, source synthesis and audience-sensitive register. |
| C1 | CEFR level | Richtgraad 4 | Flexible, precise communication on demanding subjects. |
| pre-C2 | Internal C1 → C2 bridge | No equivalent module asserted | Nuance, ambiguity and adaptable mediation. |
| C2 | CEFR level; advanced extension | No equivalent module verified | Fine distinctions, complex synthesis and purposeful style. |

The ministry's Richtgraad 1 profile includes Breakthrough and Waystage and ends
at A2. Richtgraad 2 leads toward B1 through multiple modules; it should not be
treated as one short jump from A2. Richtgraden 3 and 4 map to B2 and C1. The
published profiles also describe text characteristics, domains, roles and
communication strategies. Those conditions matter alongside the skill itself.

Institutional course numbers require their own mapping. For example, KU Leuven
ILT currently calls its first Dutch course **A2** and its second **A2+**. A
provider's “level 1” must never be automatically converted to CEFR A1.

## Four skills in every stage

`content/alignment/levels.json` stores a short Dutch, English and Persian target
for each skill, an original task suggestion, support conditions, provisional
language targets and the relevant source IDs. The following examples describe
the progression; they are product designs rather than copied official tasks.

| Band | Reading | Listening | Speaking | Writing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-A1 | Match a shop sign to a picture. | Identify a slowly repeated number. | Give a prepared greeting and name. | Enter personal details. |
| A1 | Locate a day and place in an invitation. | Select a time from a clear reminder. | Ask for an appointment and repeat the time. | Send a brief request. |
| A2 | Compare a reminder with available times. | Identify the action required by a voicemail. | Explain a conflict and confirm a workable arrangement. | Write a clear change request. |
| B1 | Combine a notice and an email to plan a response. | Take notes and identify what to do next. | Explain a problem, negotiate and clarify. | Write a complaint with facts and a solution. |
| B2 | Compare the arguments in two reports. | Distinguish positions in a discussion. | Present a proposal and address objections. | Write an audience-appropriate argument. |
| C1 | Interpret assumptions across demanding texts. | Synthesize positions and implicit links. | Explain a complex issue and adapt register. | Write a coherent source-based synthesis. |
| C2 | Compare plausible interpretations. | Process a complex discussion after familiarisation. | Mediate disagreement with precise distinctions. | Edit a complex text for contrasting audiences. |

Bridge stages change the demands of the task: less scaffolding, more connected
discourse, a different recipient or one additional uncertainty. Reusing a useful
word is appropriate; reusing an entire paragraph without a changed purpose does
not demonstrate a higher level.

The grammar lists in the data are **provisional instructional choices**. They
are not presented as a complete official grammar syllabus. Each lesson still
needs a contract that identifies its actual vocabulary, structures, functions,
scenario facts, task outcome and accepted alternatives. A competent reviewer
must check the lesson's Dutch and difficulty against that contract.

Keep the learner's four skill records separate. Record support such as a hint,
translation, sample answer or writing coach. A later independent response in a
changed context is stronger evidence than a corrected immediate retry. Course
checks can identify useful next practice; their scores cannot issue an official
level claim or recognised certificate. Every stage remains accessible.

## Belgian exam preparation catalog

`content/alignment/exams.json` contains sourced **readiness blueprints**. It does
not contain copied exam papers or validated mock scores. The official exam
holders remain responsible for dates, eligibility, permitted tools, assessment
and certificates. This is a catalog of relevant Dutch-language routes, not an
exhaustive registry of every Belgian language examination.

| Route | Verified reference | What the app can practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NT2 end-of-course checks | Provider-specific courses and four-skill assessment | A checklist against the learner's actual course contract; original practice in each skill. |
| Public certifying tests | A1/A2 and B1/B2 routes at public holders | Everyday or public-service tasks, with the selected holder's current format linked. |
| CNaVT INFO | A2, informal everyday context | Understand a request and respond with necessary details. |
| CNaVT FORM | B1, formal social context | Explain an issue and request a practical solution. |
| CNaVT PROF | B2, professional context | Summarise a workplace problem and discuss action. |
| CNaVT STRT | B2, higher-education entry context | Take notes, compare study information and explain a recommendation. |
| CNaVT EDUP | C1, demanding educational/professional context | Synthesize positions and defend a nuanced conclusion. |
| ITNA B2 | Computer components followed by eligible speaking | Original language-in-use, reading and listening practice; presentation and argumentation. |
| ITNA C1 | Qualifying computer/speaking results plus follow-on writing | Advanced original writing and revision; explain the real procedure. |
| BOSA professional tests | Certificate- and legal-article-specific modules | A workplace rehearsal after identifying the precise certificate requirements. |

Important format differences:

- The Taalunie confirms five CNaVT profiles across A2–C1. CLL Brussels describes
  listening-related tasks, reading/writing and individual spoken interaction.
  VUB ACTO's May 2026 offering confirms STRT B2 and EDUP C1. This does not establish
  a 2027 date or availability at every university.
- ITNA B2 has a computer test covering language in use, reading and listening,
  followed by speaking when the computer result is sufficient. Speaking includes
  a presentation and argumentation. C1 is an eligible follow-on writing procedure,
  not a separate generic four-part exam. Its official writing instructions permit
  Word spelling and grammar checking.
- Atlas describes written and oral test parts. The Brussels holder describes
  reading/listening first, then writing/speaking on another day. Do not impose a
  single shared timetable on all public certifying tests. Atlas explicitly does
  not organise C1 tests.
- University availability is specific. UAntwerpen Linguapolis explicitly says it
  stopped administering CNaVT in 2020; it continues to offer ITNA. KU Leuven ILT,
  UGent UCT and VUB ACTO also publish ITNA information. Admission acceptance must
  be checked for the actual programme, not inferred from an app result.
- BOSA uses certificate-specific modules and rules. No universal BOSA level,
  four-part format or pass score is asserted here.

### C2 and integration coverage limits

No currently bookable ITNA or CNaVT C2 exam has been verified for this catalog.
UGent's 2026–2027 regulations recognise a C2 certificate from a Belgian or Dutch
university language centre. Recognition is not proof that a particular centre
currently offers a C2 test. C2 activities therefore remain an expert-review
extension of the CEFR, with no invented exam provider or accreditation.

Flemish integration and Dutch-language evidence requirements must be kept
separate from the Netherlands' integration exams. The June 2026 Flemish
announcement about B1 oral describes a first-principle approval of proposed
changes; that announcement alone does not establish the effective rule for a
particular learner. Link the competent authority rather than displaying an
unverified eligibility verdict. The Netherlands' Staatsexamen NT2 is a separate
system, although Belgian institutions may accept a particular certificate.

## Dutch variety and content quality

Production targets use Belgian Standard Dutch. A standard expression shared
with the Netherlands is not an error merely because a Belgian alternative also
exists. The lexicon should identify meaning, context, register and source-backed
regional labels; it must not turn a preference into a blanket ban.

For example, `voormiddag` and `namiddag` require explanation because their usual
time reference differs between Belgium and the Netherlands. Their interpretation
must be checked in the sentence and calendar context. A word-pair checklist
cannot establish that every sentence is natural Belgian Dutch.

From B1, include labelled listening exposure to informal and regional Dutch
when suitable for the task. Keep those receptive examples distinct from formal
production targets. Successful communication does not require a native accent;
text transcripts cannot establish accent, pronunciation or prosody quality.

External resources such as NedBox can provide authentic listening and reading
practice. Link to their activities and record the learner's reflection; do not
copy videos, newspapers or coursebook content without the necessary rights.

## Review and maintenance

The loader checks twelve-stage ordering, four-skill coverage, source provenance,
bridge labels, Flemish mappings and the absence of invented C2 certification.
Those tests validate the **record contract**, not educational effectiveness.

Before describing a content pack as reviewed, record a competent human review
tied to that pack's content hash. Review language naturalness, task answerability,
required support and appropriate difficulty separately from software tests.
Retain learner observations as a separate source of evidence. Source checks
must be renewed when formats, availability or admission rules change.

This release does not rewrite or approve the existing content banks. It supplies
traceable expectations that can be used to audit and improve them in manageable
packs.
